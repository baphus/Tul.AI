# Official scholarship research — 14 August 2026

Research scope: provider-owned government, LGU, and university pages plus their
official PDFs only (Tier 1/2). This is a cataloguing brief, not an assertion that
an application is open. The status below is as checked on 2026-08-14; an expired
or periodic call must be rechecked before it is published as `Verified`.

## Recommended additional records

### 1. CHED Merit Scholarship Program (CMSP)

- **Provider / source:** Commission on Higher Education; [official program page](https://ched.gov.ph/merit-scholarship) (Tier 1) and [CMO No. 13 s. 2025](https://ched.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/CMO-NO.-13-S.-2025.pdf) (Tier 2).
- **Status:** the published AY 2025–2026 call closed 20 June 2025; **Needs Verification** for the next cycle.
- **Benefit:** full or half scholarship. The official page publishes, for full scholars, up to PHP120,000/year in private HEIs (PHP40,000 tuition, PHP70,000 stipend, PHP10,000 book/connectivity) or PHP80,000/year in SUCs/LUCs (free higher education plus the published allowances).
- **Eligibility fields:** Filipino; incoming/current first-year undergraduate; Philippines SHS graduate; GWA at least 93%; combined annual parent/guardian income at most PHP500,000; CHED priority programme at an eligible HEI; no concurrent national government-funded scholarship.
- **Documents:** online form, PSA/NSO birth certificate, signed SF9/Form 138, one income proof (ITR, BIR non-filer proof, OFW proof, or social case study), and applicable equity/guardianship documents.

### 2. Bagong Pilipinas Merit Scholarship Program (BPMSP)

- **Provider / source:** CHED; [official CHED Region XII posting](https://region12.ched.gov.ph/postings), [application portal](https://bpms.ched.gov.ph), and [priority lists](https://bpms.ched.gov.ph/priority-lists) (Tier 1).
- **Status:** the posting says 30 April–30 June 2026 or until slots were filled; **Expired** as of this research date.
- **Benefit:** 20,000 national slots are announced by [CHED's programme launch](https://ched.gov.ph/president-marcos-leads-launch-of-key-education-initiatives-with-pbbm-gabay-ng-bayan-programs/); monetary package is **To Confirm** from the next call.
- **Eligibility fields:** Filipino; incoming first-year; top 5 of class or GWA at least 95%; no previous college units; annual family income at most PHP2,000,000; CHED priority course.
- **Documents:** online-upload documents are required; specific checklist **To Confirm** from the next call.

### 3. CHED SIKAP (Scholarships for Staff and Instructors' Knowledge Advancement Program)

- **Provider / source:** CHED; [SIKAP portal](https://sikap.ched.gov.ph/) and [updated CMO No. 9 s. 2025 guidelines](https://ched.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/UPDATED_CMO_NO_9_S_2025_Updated_Guidelines_for_SIKAP.pdf) (Tier 1/2).
- **Status:** **Periodic / Needs Verification**. The portal states individuals do not directly apply; a Sending Institution nominates applicants.
- **Benefit:** published full-time support includes actual tuition, PHP41,000/month (master's) or PHP54,000/month (doctorate), plus research support of PHP150,000/PHP250,000. Published part-time support includes actual tuition plus PHP40,000/term subsidy and the applicable research support.
- **Eligibility fields:** Filipino full-time teaching/non-teaching personnel of a CHED-recognized HEI, government institution, or MOU institution; admitted to/enrolling in an eligible MA/PhD; health/mental fitness. SIKAP-N is for non-regular personnel with at least five years at the sending HEI.
- **Documents:** institution nomination, service/employment evidence, admission/enrolment evidence, fitness certification, and clearance/no-other-government-scholarship evidence; exact active-call checklist **To Confirm**.

### 4. OWWA Congressional Migrant Workers Scholarship Program (CMWSP)

- **Provider / source:** Overseas Workers Welfare Administration; [official scholarship portal](https://scholarship.owwa.gov.ph/) (Tier 1).
- **Status:** **Needs Verification**; the portal does not publish a current deadline.
- **Benefit:** maximum PHP60,000 per school year through a 4–5-year Philippine degree; funded through a PCSO allocation.
- **Eligibility fields:** deserving migrant worker or immediate descendant; priority S&T field; Philippine 4–5-year baccalaureate degree.
- **Documents:** **To Confirm** from the active OWWA call.

### 5. OWWA Education and Livelihood Assistance Program (ELAP) — college support

- **Provider / source:** OWWA; [official ELAP page](https://owwa.gov.ph/education-and-livelihood-assistance-program-elap-2/) (Tier 1).
- **Status:** ongoing benefit page; current intake mechanics/deadline **Needs Verification**.
- **Benefit:** up to PHP10,000 per school year for an eligible college dependent; the programme also provides surviving-family livelihood support up to PHP15,000.
- **Eligibility fields:** dependent of an OFW who was an active OWWA member at death, or dependent of an OFW incarcerated/convicted and given the death penalty abroad with at least one OWWA contribution.
- **Documents:** **To Confirm** with the responsible OWWA Regional Welfare Office before publication.

### 6. Cebu Province CP-GIFTS tertiary education grant

- **Provider / source:** Province of Cebu; [official 2026 launch announcement](https://www.cebu.gov.ph/12954/capitol-launches-new-season-of-cp-gifts-scholarship-program-with-%E2%82%B159m-funding/) (Tier 1).
- **Status:** 2026 season launched 9 June; announcement reports open slots but gives no closing date — **Needs Verification**.
- **Benefit:** PHP10,000 financial assistance to deserving college students; 5,900 2026 slots, with 2,500 Governor, 1,500 Vice Governor, and 1,900 district allocations.
- **Eligibility fields:** college student; relevant constituency/district allocation where applicable. The announcement says students from any part of Cebu Province may qualify through the Governor/Vice Governor offices; other screening fields **To Confirm**.
- **Documents:** **To Confirm** with the Provincial School Board/PESO before publishing.

### 7. Cebu Capitol Medical Scholarship (CNU–VSMMC College of Medicine)

- **Provider / source:** Province of Cebu; [official programme details](https://www.cebu.gov.ph/10394/cebu-launches-practice-based-residency-program-to-retain-capitol-doctors/) and [benefit announcement](https://www.cebu.gov.ph/10383/gov-pam-guarantees-merit-based-selection-for-cebu-capitol-medical-scholars/) (Tier 1).
- **Status:** the 2026 application window was February–June; **Expired**. Recheck future intakes.
- **Benefit:** Province shoulders published CNU–VSMMC tuition: PHP74,000 first year, PHP76,500 second year, and at least PHP78,000 third/fourth years.
- **Eligibility fields:** four-year degree, Cebu Province residency, qualifying GWA, NMAT, interview, entrance examination; preference for applicants from LGUs with critical doctor shortages; return-of-service is part of the scholarship pathway.
- **Documents:** degree/academic record, Cebu residency evidence, NMAT, and medical-school application evidence are implied by the published requirements; exact checklist **To Confirm**.

### 8. Cebu Technological University internally funded academic scholarships

- **Provider / source:** Cebu Technological University; [official scholarship page](https://www.ctu.edu.ph/main/academics/scholarships/) and [official Citizen's Charter checklist](https://www.ctu.edu.ph/carmen/wp-content/uploads/sites/45/2025/07/CC-2024-Consolidated.pdf) (Tier 1/2).
- **Status:** **Periodic / Needs Verification**; CTU describes application via Google Form and documentary requirements but no current deadline.
- **Benefit:** published internal awards include 50% tuition discounts (BOT 506/BOT 530) and free tuition/miscellaneous fees (BOT 294/BOR #9/BOR #28). Exact award eligibility varies by resolution.
- **Eligibility fields:** CTU student who meets the particular award's qualifications; financial assistance may require low-income/indigency evidence.
- **Documents:** application form; ITR, barangay certificate of indigency/low income or equivalent as required; sponsor/benefactor documents where applicable.
